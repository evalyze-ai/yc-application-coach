## Form structure (public capture, Fall 2026, July 23, 2026)

Sections in order: Founders (profiles; how long known; who writes code; looking for a cofounder), Founder Video, Company (name; 50-character description; URL; demo; product link; login credentials; what are you going to make; where do you live now and where after YC; explain location), Progress (how far along; how long and full-time; tech stack with AI models and coding tools; are people using it; revenue; previous batch or pivot; incubators and accelerators), Idea (why this idea; competitors and insight; how you'll make money and how much; other ideas), Equity (legal entity; investment; fundraising), Curious (what convinced you; how you heard), Batch Preference.

Source: github.com/withkynam/YC-application-tentative (a founder's paste of their own form page). Yes/no questions open follow-up fields that the capture doesn't show.
