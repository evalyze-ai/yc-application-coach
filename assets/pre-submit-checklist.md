# Pre-submit checklist

Mark each line Ready, Needs work or Unknown. A full set of Ready doesn't predict acceptance; it means the application is easy to assess.

## Clarity
- [ ] A stranger can repeat the one-liner, and it names a user and a job (50 characters or fewer, spaces counted).
- [ ] "What is your company going to make?" lets a reader picture the product after one read.
- [ ] The first sentence of every answer stands on its own.
- [ ] Someone outside the field retold the company story correctly in three sentences.
- [ ] Every part of every multipart question is answered.

## Evidence
- [ ] Every progress claim has a number and a date or period. Nothing is rounded up or silently annualized.
- [ ] Built, planned and done-by-hand are clearly separated.
- [ ] Users, active users, paying customers, pilots, LOIs and waitlist are kept distinct.
- [ ] Revenue is shown month by month with currency, recurring and one-time separated.
- [ ] At least one fact shows someone outside the team paying, depending on, or repeatedly using the product (or the stage honestly says it's not there yet).
- [ ] "Who writes code" names a founder and what they built; any outside work is disclosed with ownership.
- [ ] Each founder profile has one specific, checkable achievement.

## Judgment
- [ ] The competitor answer names real alternatives (including spreadsheet, in-house, incumbent, doing nothing) and gives one earned insight.
- [ ] If the category is crowded, "we've seen this before" is answered.
- [ ] The money answer is short and bottom-up, with a step to a bigger market.
- [ ] One to three serious other ideas are listed, or the field is deliberately blank.

## Team and commitment
- [ ] The founders' history gives a year, a context and something shipped together.
- [ ] Every founder's full-time date is stated.
- [ ] The equity split is near-equal or explained in one sentence that holds up.
- [ ] Prior funding, other accelerators, employer IP agreements and non-founder code are disclosed exactly.

## Consistency
- [ ] Product and first customer match across the one-liner, product answer, video and demo.
- [ ] Founders, roles and availability match across profiles, team answers and equity.
- [ ] Start, full-time and pivot dates match across progress, profiles and any previous application.
- [ ] User and revenue numbers use the same units, definitions and as-of dates everywhere.
- [ ] Any update you've sent hasn't made another answer stale.

## Video, demo and access
- [ ] The founder video is about a minute, every founder speaks, from bullets, only founders talking, under the file limit.
- [ ] The demo shows the real product doing its core job; simulated or manual steps are labeled.
- [ ] Product link and credentials work logged out, with sample data loaded and no real customer data.
- [ ] Optional AI-building evidence is attached only if it shows disciplined real work.

## Voice and final pass
- [ ] It reads like the founders wrote it: no em dashes, no "wedge," no "leverage," no "It's not X, it's Y."
- [ ] Every founder could defend every sentence in the interview without notes.
- [ ] Two or three readers have reviewed it, and it was cut after their feedback.
- [ ] This batch's deadline and form were checked on YC's own pages.
- [ ] Every founder profile is complete, and you saved a copy of what you submitted.
